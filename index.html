Services

What I take off your desk.

Five areas of work, covering almost everything a small business, landlord, creator or self‑employed client needs from an accountant.

01

Self Assessment & personal tax

If you are self‑employed, earn rental income or have income HMRC does not tax at source, a return has to be filed — accurately, and on time.

How I help I prepare and file your return, check the expenses and reliefs you are entitled to, and tell you what you owe and when — well before the deadline, not the week of it.
02

Limited company accounts & corporation tax

Directors answer to both Companies House and HMRC, with separate deadlines and penalties that begin the day one is missed.

How I help I prepare and file your annual accounts and corporation tax return, keep your filing dates in hand, and talk you through what the numbers actually mean for the year ahead.
03

Landlord & property accounts

Property income has rules of its own — allowable expenses, finance cost restrictions, and different treatment depending on how the property is held.

How I help I handle rental income and property company reporting, and make sure you are claiming everything you are entitled to and nothing that would not stand up to scrutiny.
04

Bookkeeping, VAT & payroll

Day‑to‑day compliance is where most of the stress lives, and where small errors quietly become expensive ones.

How I help I keep your records current, prepare and file VAT returns, and run payroll, so your obligations are met without you having to chase them.
05

Influencers, creators & online businesses

Brand deals, affiliate income, ad revenue from overseas platforms and gifted products all carry tax consequences — and gifted items are rarely as free as they look.

How I help I work out whether you are better off as a sole trader or a limited company, handle income from UK and overseas platforms, deal with VAT once you cross the threshold, and make sure paid partnerships and gifting are declared the way HMRC expects.
