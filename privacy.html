Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to the personal information you give me through this website, and the rights you have over it.

Who is responsible for your information

Nicole Platten Accounting, Birmingham, United Kingdom, is the data controller for information submitted through this website. If you have any question about this notice, email nicoleplatten@outlook.com or call 07492 990955.

What I collect and why

If you use the enquiry form, I collect the name, email address, telephone number (if you give one), the service you selected and the message you write. I use this only to reply to your enquiry and, if you go on to become a client, to provide the accounting services you have asked for.

If you contact me by telephone, email, WhatsApp or LinkedIn instead, I hold the details you choose to share in those messages, for the same purpose.

I do not use your details for marketing, and I never sell or rent them to anyone.

My lawful basis

For responding to an enquiry, the lawful basis is legitimate interests — specifically, replying to someone who has asked me to get in touch. Where you become a client, information is processed to perform our contract and to meet the legal and regulatory obligations that apply to accountancy work, including anti‑money laundering record keeping.

Who else handles it

  • Formspree processes submissions from the enquiry form and forwards them to my email inbox.
  • Microsoft (Outlook) hosts the email account those messages arrive in.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and keeps standard server logs.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on these pages. Your browser requests those font files directly from Google, which will see your IP address as part of that request.

Some of these providers operate outside the UK. Where that is the case, transfers are covered by the safeguards those providers put in place, such as the UK extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies, and there is no analytics, advertising or tracking software on it.

How long it is kept

Enquiries that do not lead to any engagement are deleted once the conversation has clearly ended and there is no reason to keep them. Client records are kept for as long as the professional and legal retention requirements that apply to accountancy work require, and then deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information I hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to ask for it in a portable format. To exercise any of these, email nicoleplatten@outlook.com and I will respond within one month.

If you are not satisfied with how I have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
